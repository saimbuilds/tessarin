Every word on the site, in scroll order, plus the rules behind it. The words live in `index.html`; edit them there and keep this file in step.

## Voice

1. **Say what we build and what the client gets.** Concrete nouns, short sentences, no riddles.
2. **Write to "you".** The client's goal is the subject of the sentence, not our talent.
3. **Prove it, don't claim it.** Trust comes from specifics: you talk to the people building it, you see it working every week, our AI says so when it does not know.
4. **Confident, never loud.** No exclamation marks, no "best in class", no promises we can't measure.
5. **No em dashes.** Use a full stop, a comma or a colon.
6. **No location line.** We work with companies anywhere in the world.
7. **Banned words:** elevate, seamless, cutting-edge, innovative, unlock, leverage, solutions, digital experiences, next-level, game-changer, synergy, empower, revolutionise.
8. **Fit the type.** Uppercase display headings run very large, so the uppercase part should be 8 letters a word or fewer to fit on a phone. The italic part carries the turn of the line.
9. **Honest samples.** The three projects stay flagged "Sample" until real case studies replace them.

## Why these lines

- **"Future, assembled."** stays: the loader assembles the pieces, the hero names what just happened, and it is the studio's name (tessera, a mosaic tile) in two words.
- **The manifesto** answers the three fears every client has about a studio: will it be hard enough for them, will I be handed to a junior, will I see nothing for weeks.
- **Each service** leads with the result (enquiries, work off your team, a showroom with nothing to download) and keeps the technology in the tags, where technical buyers look for it.
- **"Built to say so when it does not know"** is the line that makes a careful buyer trust the AI work, because it names the risk they are worried about.
- **The process** uses real time markers (Day 01, Day 03, Week 02) so the speed is a fact, not an adjective.
- **"30 minutes with the people who will build it"** removes the last doubt before the call: it is short, and it is not a sales call.
